About GlobalRemote

GlobalRemote is an independent global job discovery directory dedicated to aggregating verified remote work platforms with zero geographic barriers. We emphasize high-growth digital sectors, including machine learning model evaluation, RLHF prompt writing, data annotation, micro-task crowdsourcing (Toloka, Clickworker, Populi, Claru), general software freelancing, technical copywriting, and digital research.

Our editorial team reviews international hiring platforms daily to index entry-level micro-tasks and specialized contract opportunities, connecting global job seekers with verified hiring portals.
